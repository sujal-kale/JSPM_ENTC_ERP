Who We Are

Vision & Mission

Fostering and strengthening lifelong ties between our alumni and the institution.

🌍

Our Vision

To create a global community through commitment, value, and support to empower the future of JSCOE and its Alumni.

🎯

Our Mission

To foster and strengthen ties and create a mutually beneficial relationship between Alumni and New Horizon by providing diverse benefits — including networking opportunities, career development, special events, and the occasion to connect with and inspire students. To also engage with New Horizon's future goals, such that each individual feels welcome and valued as a member of the Alumni community.

Why It Matters

Importance of Alumni Relations

Alumni relations are an essential part of an institution's advancement activities — for many reasons.

1

Alumni are an institution's most loyal supporters.

2

Alumni are valuable fundraising prospects.

3

Alumni generate invaluable word-of-mouth marketing among their social and professional networks.

4

By engaging alumni, an institution continues to benefit from their skills and experience.

5

Alumni are great role models for current students and offer practical support as they start their careers.

6

Alumni are often in a position to engage the expertise of the institution in their professional lives.

7

Our alumni are our international ambassadors — carrying our knowledge into their professional and social networks worldwide.

Governance

Standard Operating Procedures

Guidelines for managing the alumni database and association effectively and efficiently. Click each item to expand.

1 Purpose ▼
  • The purpose of this SOP is to establish guidelines for managing the alumni database and association effectively and efficiently.
2 Scope ▼
  • This SOP applies to all personnel involved in managing the alumni database and association, including database administrators, coordinators, and Alumni members of the association.
3 Alumni Registration ▼
  • The registration form shall be available on the portal and in the respective departments, and registration shall remain open throughout the year. An Alumni Digital ID Card shall be issued after successful registration.
  • All outgoing students must compulsorily enter all required details along with their photo on the Alumni Portal.
  • A mechanism for data modification is provided to alumni to change their contact number, profile details, and email address throughout the year.
4 Membership Fee ▼
  • Registration fees for memberships to the Alumni Association will be collected from the final semester of Engineering students.
5 Data Privacy & Security ▼
  • Protect alumni data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse by implementing access controls, encryption, and regular security audits.
  • No data is to be shared with anyone without the President's and other Alumni members' permission.
6 Communication with Alumni ▼
  • Members will be kept informed about institution and Association activities regularly through an alumni notice board on the portal.
  • Every event shall have a poster with details, posted on social media and the Alumni portal well in advance, with mails sent to registered e-mail IDs. Reminders will be posted on the Alumni WhatsApp group department-wise.
7 Alumni Activities ▼
  • The Association shall plan activities regularly, including Alumni Talks, Alumni Meet, Entrepreneurs Meet, Achievement Recognition, Job Mela, Cultural Fest, Alumni Relive (one-day college life), Sports Day, Social Awareness / Extension Activities, Career Guidance Seminars, Motivational Talks, and Mentoring.
8 Maintaining Accounts ▼
  • A separate bank account is maintained for Alumni Association activities.
9 Alumni Association Committee ▼
  • Committee Members' tenure is restricted to 3 years. Subsequently, the new Committee shall be formed by election under the supervision of the President–Alumni Association.
  • The committee consists of eight members — President, Vice President, Secretary, Joint-Secretary, Treasurer, and ten department-wise Board members.
  • Any member may be disqualified or removed when found conducting activities harmful or not in the interest of the Association.
10 Committee – Roles & Responsibilities ▼
  • Serve as a liaison between the Alma mater and Alumni Community.
  • Work with the President and staff coordinator to build a strong Alumni Association.
  • Coordinate guest talks, motivational talks, career development talks, and higher-studies guidance for current students.
  • Register alumni of older batches not yet in the association.
  • Bring new ideas to keep alumni constantly in touch.
  • Identify alumni as jury members for co/extracurricular activities.
  • Organize social awareness programs among the community.
  • Maintain an up-to-date alumni database with contact, biographical, and career info.
  • Connect industry and institution to benefit students.
  • Plan events like Alumni Meet/reunions, Entrepreneurs Meet, Job Mela, Sports, Recognition banquet, and more.
  • Identify mentors and placement support among former students.
  • Support financially poor students and raise funds for them.
  • Plan the Best Outgoing Student Award and raise alumni funds.
  • Plan meetings at least once every 6 months.
  • Review and recommend candidates for the alumni recognition award.
  • Adopt core values of excellence, lifelong relationships, lifelong learning, and advocacy.
  • Raise funds through alumni contributions or sponsorships.
11 Alumni Coordinator – Roles & Responsibilities ▼
  • Coordinate alumni-delivered guest, motivational, career development, and higher-studies talks for current batches.
  • Identify and honour alumni who achieved professional growth.
  • Register alumni of older batches not yet in the association.
  • Educate graduating students about alumni benefits and engage them.
  • Never share alumni data without President's permission.
  • Bring new ideas to keep alumni in touch with the organization.
  • Include alumni as DAB members and invite them to DAB meetings.
  • Invite alumni as jury members for college/department activities.
  • Invite alumni to be part of accreditation activities as required.
  • Organize alumni meetings with the President.
  • Respond to alumni questions, concerns, and suggestions.
  • Build relationships with alumni locally, regionally, nationally, and internationally.
12 Roles & Responsibilities of Office Bearers ▼
President
  • Presides over Executive Committee meetings and generally supervises the functions of the Association.
  • Ensures implementation of the SoP (Annexure – I).
Vice-President
  • Assists the President and discharges the President's duties in his/her absence.
Secretary
  • Maintains minutes of the meetings.
  • Informs all members about activities as planned and approved by the President.
Joint Secretary
  • Assists the Secretary and carries out functions in his/her absence.
Treasurer
  • Maintains the account and submits the Statement of Account at the end of the Financial Year.
Faculty Co-ordinators
  • Help plan/organize events, guide the committee, and share details with alumni department-wise.
13 Meetings of JSCOE Alumni Association ▼
A. Executive Committee
  • Meets from time to time as advised by the President on clear notice of seven days.
  • Patrons may convene an emergent meeting on at least three days' prior notice.
  • Quorum is four (4); decisions are by majority vote.
  • If quorum is insufficient, the meeting is adjourned for half an hour, after which no quorum is required.
B. Alumni Meet
  • Conducted once a year (or as suggested by the President) on the JSCOE campus with 10 days' clear notice.
  • Prior approval is taken by the Executive Committee from the management.

Roles & Responsibilities

The committee consists of eight members serving a three-year tenure.

President

Presides over Executive Committee meetings, supervises the functions of the Association, and ensures implementation of the SoP.

Vice-President

Assists the President in official duties and discharges the President's responsibilities in his/her absence.

Secretary

Maintains minutes of meetings and informs all members about activities planned and approved by the President.

Joint Secretary

Assists the Secretary in all matters and carries out functions in his/her absence.

Treasurer

Maintains the account of the Association and submits the Statement of Account at the end of the financial year.

Faculty Co-ordinators

Help plan and organize events, guide the committee, and share details with alumni department-wise.

Protocol

Meetings of the Association

Executive Committee

  • Meets as advised by the President on seven days' clear notice.
  • Patrons may convene emergent meetings on three days' notice.
  • Quorum is four (4); decisions are by majority vote.
  • On insufficient quorum, the meeting adjourns for half an hour, after which no quorum is required.

Alumni Meet

  • Conducted once a year (or as suggested by the President) on the JSCOE campus.
  • Held on a clear notice of 10 days.
  • Prior approval is taken by the Executive Committee from the management.
