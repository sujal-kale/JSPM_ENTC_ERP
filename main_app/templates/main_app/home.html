Department Of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering

About the Department

Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering education and research, fostering innovation and entrepreneurship.

Mission

To impart quality education, promote research in emerging technologies, and develop skilled professionals for industry and society.

The Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at JSCOE offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs with a focus on cutting-edge areas like VLSI, Wireless Communications, Signal Processing, and IoT.

Academic Programs

B.Tech. ECE

4-year undergraduate program in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

M.Tech. VLSI

2-year postgraduate program specializing in Very Large Scale Integration.

M.Tech. Communication Engg.

Advanced studies in wireless and optical communication systems.

Ph.D. Programs

Research opportunities in electronics, telecom, and related fields.

Research Areas

Our department is at the forefront of research in electronics and telecom. Explore our labs and publications.

VLSI Design Lab

Focus on chip design, FPGA prototyping, and semiconductor research.

Wireless Communications Lab

Research in 5G, IoT, and antenna design.

Signal Processing Lab

Advanced algorithms for image, audio, and biomedical signals.
